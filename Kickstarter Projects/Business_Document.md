# Kickstarter Success Analysis – Business Document

## Business Problem

Kickstarter is one of the world's largest crowdfunding platforms where creators launch projects and raise funds from backers. However, a significant number of projects fail to reach their funding goals. Understanding the factors that contribute to project success can help creators plan more effective crowdfunding campaigns.

This project analyzes historical Kickstarter data to identify trends in project performance, funding behavior, category popularity, country-wise distribution, and backer engagement using Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI.

---

# Project Objectives

* Analyze Kickstarter project performance.
* Compare successful and failed projects.
* Identify the most popular project categories.
* Analyze country-wise project distribution.
* Study funding goals and pledged amounts.
* Analyze backer participation.
* Build an interactive business dashboard.
* Generate business insights to support better decision-making.

# Key Business Insights

* The United States has the highest number of Kickstarter projects.
* Film & Video is the most popular project category.
* Dance has one of the highest project success rates.
* Games attract the highest average number of backers.
* Successful projects receive significantly more backers than failed projects.
* Some projects have extremely high funding goals, indicating the presence of outliers.
* Category selection and backer engagement strongly influence project success.

---

# Business Recommendations

* Focus on project categories with historically higher success rates.
* Set realistic funding goals.
* Increase marketing efforts before campaign launch.
* Encourage early backer engagement.
* Study successful campaigns to identify best practices.
* Use historical data to make informed crowdfunding decisions.
